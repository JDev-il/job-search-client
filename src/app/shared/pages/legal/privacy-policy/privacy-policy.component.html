{{ legal.appName }} ({{ legal.appUrl }}) is a tool that helps you keep track of the jobs you apply for. It is operated by {{ legal.operator }} ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the service collects, what we do with it, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, write to us at {{ legal.contactEmail }}.

1. Information we collect

Account information.

  • If you register with an email and password: your name, your email address and your password. The password is stored only in hashed form.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, your email address and your Google account identifier.

Information you add to the service.

  • The job applications you track (company, role, status, dates, notes) and the job-search preferences you enter.

Your browser.

  • A sign-in token stored in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

2. How we use it

To run the service: to sign you in, to show and update your applications, to provide the features described below, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Gmail access

{{ legal.appName }} can connect to your Gmail account so it can notice emails about your applications (an interview invitation, a rejection, an offer) and keep your tracker up to date. Google asks for your permission when you sign in with Google or connect Gmail from your account page.

  • Read-only. We request only the gmail.readonly permission. We cannot send, delete or change any of your email.
  • What we read. When a new message arrives, Google notifies us (through Google Cloud Pub/Sub; the notification contains only your email address and a mailbox change number). We then fetch the new message's sender, subject, text and identifiers.
  • What we do with it. We work out whether the message is about one of your applications and, if so, suggest or apply a status change in your tracker. Many changes wait for your confirmation first.
  • What we keep. Your Gmail address and the access credentials Google gives us for your account. For each suggestion, the message and conversation identifiers, the sender's name and domain, the subject, a short snippet (up to 240 characters) and our classification. We do not keep the full text of your emails, and we do not write email subjects or text to our logs.
  • Sent to an AI provider. When our own rules are not sure what a message means, its sender, subject and text are sent to OpenAI's API to be classified. We do not authorise OpenAI to use this data to train its models.
  • Who can see it. Our staff do not read the content of your email, except with your explicit permission, where needed to investigate abuse or a security problem, or where the law requires it.
{{ legal.appName }}'s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping Gmail access. You can disconnect Gmail at any time from the account page. That deletes the credentials and Gmail details we hold for you and stops all further reading of your mailbox. You can also remove our access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. If Google tells us your permission has expired or been revoked, we delete the stored credentials automatically and ask you to connect again.

4. AI assistant

If you use the assistant feature, the message you type and the job-search preferences you have entered are sent to OpenAI's API to produce a response.

These providers process information only to provide their service to us. Some of them (Google, OpenAI) may process data outside the European Economic Area. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

7. Security
